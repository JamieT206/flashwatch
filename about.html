About FlashWatch

FlashWatch warns farmers of flash droughts one to two weeks ahead and works out whether extra irrigation pays. It covers the Corn Belt and northern Plains: 143 million acres of corn and soybeans.

How it works

  1. Soil and weather now. ERA5-Land soil moisture, rain and heat for each 28 km square, as known on the forecast date.
  2. Weather ahead. ECMWF's extended-range forecasts: 11 runs, each a possible next four weeks.
  3. The forecast. A machine-learning model (LightGBM) gives the chance a flash drought begins in the next 7 days and in days 8-14. It was trained on 2005-2021 and tested on seasons it never saw.
  4. The decision. A water balance of the field (pivot, soil, crop, prices) turns that chance into dollars: start extra irrigation today, or wait for the next forecast.
  5. The agents. Meta's Muse Spark writes the morning text and plans pivot starts; Muse Voice Transcribe takes voice notes from the field. Nothing moves without the farmer's YES.
