    Your farm

    Defaults are for a typical Nebraska centre pivot. Change them to match your farm; everything updates. Saved in this browser.

    Every other day, FlashWatch forecasts the chance that the soil at your farm drops into drought in the next one and two weeks. A water-balance model of your field turns days of warning into dollars. This page replays past seasons, using forecasts from models that never saw that year. Data: ERA5-Land (Copernicus Climate Change Service), US Drought Monitor (NDMC, USDA, NOAA), USDA NASS, US Census Gazetteer.
