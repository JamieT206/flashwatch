    Defaults are typical published values for a Nebraska centre pivot (FAO-56 water balance, FAO-33 yield response). Replace them with your own: the advice and dollar figures update instantly.

    How it works: every other day FlashWatch forecasts the chance that root-zone soil moisture crashes into drought (a "flash drought") at your location in the next one and two weeks. A water-balance model of your field turns days of warning into dollars, and the decision weighs starting today against waiting for a fresher forecast. This page replays past seasons with forecasts from models that never saw that year. Data: ERA5-Land (Copernicus Climate Change Service), US Drought Monitor (NDMC, USDA, NOAA), USDA NASS, US Census Gazetteer.
