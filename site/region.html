How these numbers are made: every forecast comes from a model that never saw that year. The dollar figures replay the farm page's advice day by day over six past seasons, for a typical Nebraska pivot, then scale up by irrigated acres in the 2022 Census of Agriculture. They assume a flash drought in the soil record means a dry spell that would stress an irrigated crop. Calories: USDA FoodData Central (corn 365, soybeans 446 kcal per 100 g; 2,000 kcal a person a day). Retail food loss: USDA ERS, 43 billion lb in 2010. Data: ERA5-Land (Copernicus Climate Change Service), ECMWF S2S reforecasts, US Drought Monitor, USDA NASS.
