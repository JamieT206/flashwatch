Method and caveats: forecasts are out-of-fold (each year predicted by models that never saw it). The dollar figures replay the farm page's decision rule day by day over six development years, with a textbook water-balance model of a typical Nebraska pivot, and scale by the 2022 Census of Agriculture's irrigated acres. They assume a flash drought in the (rain-fed) soil record marks a dry spell that would stress an irrigated crop. Calories: USDA FoodData Central (corn 365, soybeans 446 kcal per 100 g); a person-year is 2,000 kcal a day. Retail food loss: USDA ERS, 43 billion lb in 2010. Data: ERA5-Land (Copernicus Climate Change Service), ECMWF S2S reforecasts, US Drought Monitor, USDA NASS.
