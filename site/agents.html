Agents

FlashWatch uses Meta's models (Muse Spark, Muse Glimmer, Muse Voice Transcribe and SAM 3.1) to talk with farmers by text and voice, and to connect to their farm software and equipment, especially irrigation pivots. The agents work from FlashWatch's own forecasts and dollar figures, and every figure is checked before it reaches a farmer or a pivot. Below are real runs on past seasons.

Finds your pivots

SAM 3.1

Setting up a farm shouldn't mean typing coordinates. Given the farm's location, FlashWatch fetches a USDA aerial photo, uses SAM 3.1 to find the center-pivot fields, and maps and measures each one automatically.

Connects to irrigation systems

Muse SparkWhatsApp

FlashWatch works out the best watering plan for each pivot, adjusts it for the farm's own rules and setup, checks with the farmer on WhatsApp, and then turns on the water. The water each pivot puts down is measured and fed back into the model to update its root-zone moisture estimates. Farmers can also ask FlashWatch questions and send voice notes.

Monitors weather forecasts and updates moisture models

Muse SparkWhatsApp

The watcher updates the model with each weather forecast and contacts the farmer when the drought chance or status changes, or when a new action is recommended. Here is the 2012 season for one farm, as the messages would have arrived.

Listens to the field

Muse Voice TranscribeMuse Spark

A farmer leaves a voice note from the field. Muse Voice Transcribe turns it into text; Muse Spark matches what was said to the farm's pivots and rates the crop stress. The operator agent treats a farmer-reported stressed crop as a reason to water.

Writes the weekly briefing

Muse Spark

For a county or extension office: risk this week, why, and what to watch. The agent gathers numbers through tools; a checker traces every figure in the draft back to a tool result and sends the draft back if one can't be traced.

Writes like the Drought Monitor

Muse Glimmer 30B, open weights

The US Drought Monitor's weekly narrative is the text farmers and agencies already read. FlashWatch fine-tunes Meta's open-weights Muse Glimmer on 15 years of those narratives, paired with each week's numbers, so it writes the same kind of regional summary from the data.
